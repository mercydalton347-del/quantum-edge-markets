Forex Fundamentals

Fundamental analysis considers economic information that can influence currency markets.

  • Interest rates
  • Inflation
  • Employment data
  • GDP
  • Central-bank decisions
  • Economic calendars
  • Geopolitical developments

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies historical price behaviour and market structure.

  • Support and resistance
  • Trend analysis
  • Candlestick patterns
  • Market swings
  • Indicators
  • Volume

Market Structure

Traders may study higher highs, higher lows, lower highs and lower lows to understand directional price structure.

Risk Management

ICT Concepts

2022 Framework
