Forex Education

Educational information about foreign exchange markets.

Forex Fundamentals

Fundamental analysis considers economic information that can influence currency markets.

  • Interest rates
  • Inflation
  • Employment data
  • GDP
  • Central-bank decisions
  • Economic calendars
  • Geopolitical developments

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies historical price behaviour and market structure.

  • Support and resistance
  • Trend analysis
  • Candlestick patterns
  • Market swings
  • Indicators
  • Volume

Market Structure

Traders may study higher highs, higher lows, lower highs and lower lows to understand directional price structure.

Risk Management

Position sizing, stop-loss planning and risk-to-reward analysis are important components of responsible trading education.

ICT Concepts

Educational ICT-style concepts include liquidity, market structure, fair value gaps, order blocks, dealing ranges and time-based market behaviour.

2022 Framework

Educational discussions of the ICT 2022 framework commonly examine liquidity, displacement, market structure shifts, fair value gaps and time-of-day behaviour.
