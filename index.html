Forex Education

Educational material for understanding foreign exchange markets.

Forex Fundamentals

Fundamental analysis considers economic and financial information that can influence currency prices.

  • Central-bank interest rates
  • Inflation and employment
  • GDP and economic growth
  • Economic calendars
  • Major economic releases
  • Geopolitical developments

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies price behaviour and market structure using historical market information.

  • Support and resistance
  • Trends and ranges
  • Candlestick structure
  • Market swings
  • Indicators
  • Risk management

Market Structure

Traders commonly study higher highs, higher lows, lower highs and lower lows to understand directional structure and possible changes in market behaviour.

Risk Management

Position sizing, stop-loss placement and risk-to-reward planning are important components of responsible trading education.

ICT Concepts

ICT-style educational concepts associated with Michael J. Huddleston include liquidity, market structure, fair value gaps, order blocks, dealing ranges and time-based market behaviour.

2022 Framework

Educational discussions of the ICT 2022 approach commonly examine liquidity, displacement, market structure shifts, fair value gaps and time-of-day behaviour.
